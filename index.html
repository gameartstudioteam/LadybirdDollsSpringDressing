Privacy Policy

Ladybird Dolls Spring Dressing

Last updated: October 4, 2026
Android app: com.GameArtForKids.LadybirdDollsSpringDressing

This children's doll dressing game keeps game progress and creations on the device. In the upcoming update, optional Google Analytics for Firebase is off by default and can be enabled or disabled by a parent in the parent settings. Advertising ID collection and ad personalization are disabled. The game shows ads from Google AdMob with child-directed, non-personalized settings. These services process identifiers, interactions and technical data described below; disabling personalized advertising does not mean that no user data are collected.

1. Developer and contact

GameArtForKids provides Ladybird Dolls Spring Dressing. Parents, guardians and other users can send privacy questions or requests to gameartstudioteam@gmail.com.

2. Game information kept on your device

The game saves progress, earned game coins, unlocked items, the unlocked second doll, daily-chest and gift status and language preferences locally. Saved outfits and colouring choices are stored as local game files. These records let you resume your game and keep your creations.

The game does not upload these saves to a developer-operated game server. It has no game account registration, chat, user upload service or developer cloud save account. Game coins are game rewards, not real-money purchases.

Where a game screenshot function is available, it saves an image of the game on the device. It does not photograph you or use the device camera to create that image. Images you separately copy, share or back up are subject to the services you choose.

3. Game analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Versions with analytics enabled use Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google, to measure return visits, game progress and use of game features. We use these results to maintain and improve the game. The game does not set a user account ID or send names or contact details to Analytics; advertising consent signals are denied and ad personalization signals are disabled in its configuration. Earlier versions with game analytics disabled do not send these gameplay analytics events. Advertising data processing described in section 4 may still occur in those versions.

The analytics data processed are:

  • an Analytics app-instance identifier assigned to an installation of the game, not linked by the game to a name, email address or game account; this is not the Android advertising ID. Firebase services may also process a Firebase installation identifier, which is a separate identifier;
  • automatically collected events such as first open, session start, app update and engagement time;
  • game events that contain no personal information, for example: a tutorial step finished, an outfit completed, coins earned, a helper visited, an item unlock offered, confirmed or cancelled, a gift or the daily chest opened, the second doll unlocked, a rewarded or interstitial ad shown, and the end of a session;
  • basic technical information that Google Analytics collects with every event, such as app version, operating system version, device model, language setting and approximate region derived from the IP address.

The game does not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, saved outfit images or contacts to Analytics. App-instance identifiers and associated events can nevertheless constitute personal data under applicable law. In the current Android configuration, the advertising ID permission is removed and advertising ID collection, Android ID (SSAID) collection and ad personalization signals are disabled.

Google processes analytics data to provide the service. See Google's Privacy Policy, the Firebase privacy information and the Analytics data disclosure. Data are sent over encrypted connections. Analytics retention is governed by the Analytics property's retention settings and Google's service procedures, as explained in section 7. We do not sell analytics data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google's AdMob advertising service for a banner at the bottom of the screen, interstitial ads (shown only when leaving to the map, with a minimum interval) and optional rewarded video ads. Google and its advertising service providers may process:

  • IP addresses, which can indicate an approximate location;
  • device and app information, applicable app/device identifiers (including app set ID where used by the SDK), and information about ad views, taps and other interactions;
  • diagnostics and performance information needed to operate and measure the advertising service.

These data support ad delivery, advertising measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention. Google processes them under Google's Privacy Policy. Its Mobile Ads data disclosure explains the service's data practices. Google's advertising SDK encrypts transmitted user data using TLS.

Child-directed treatment applies to every player. The game requests ads with a maximum content rating of G, treats all users as children and as under the age of consent, and shows only non-personalized ads. It does not request the Android advertising ID permission and does not ask for precise location. The game does not ask children to enter their name, date of birth, address or contact details to play. Technical data used by an advertising service are separate from local game saves.

Rewarded ads are optional and may provide game coins after an eligible ad is completed. The core game can be played without watching rewarded ads. Internet access is needed for advertisements, analytics and external webpages.

5. Parents and guardians: choices and rights

The game is designed for children. The privacy policy and the advertising privacy options are placed behind a simple parental gate in the "for parents" corner of the game map, so that a child does not open them by accident. Google's consent component (User Messaging Platform) is configured for users under the age of consent and controls whether ads may be requested. When that component requires it, the advertising privacy options can be opened from the same corner.

Parents and guardians can contact us at gameartstudioteam@gmail.com to ask about data practices or request access, correction or deletion of information we hold. We will explain what we can identify and handle through the relevant service; an email address alone does not identify an Analytics installation. Please avoid sending a child's personal details unless necessary to explain your request. Nothing in this policy limits rights that apply under the law of your country.

Uninstalling the game stops further collection by that installed copy. Android Settings, Apps, this game, Storage, Clear data removes app-private progress and resets locally stored identifiers and settings; collection may resume if you open or reinstall the game. These actions do not delete data already processed by Google.

In update 1.9, game analytics is off by default. A parent can open the parent corner, pass the parental navigation gate, read the explanation, and choose "Allow game statistics" or "Keep statistics off". The choice is saved on that device. A parent can return to disable collection at any time. Advertising availability does not enable Analytics, and gameplay events from before permission are not queued for later transmission. Refusing analytics does not change gameplay, coins or rewards. The navigation gate helps prevent accidental access; it is not identity verification or a substitute for any verifiable parental consent required by applicable law.

The parent settings also offer a privacy-request email draft and a separate, confirmed reset of local analytics data. Where an Analytics app-instance identifier is available, the draft can include that identifier to help us identify records. The parent reviews and sends the draft in their own email app. Resetting local analytics turns collection off and resets locally stored Analytics data; it keeps game progress and does not delete Google's server records. Contact us for assistance with server-data requests.

6. Request access or deletion of data

  1. A parent or guardian can email gameartstudioteam@gmail.com with the subject "Ladybird Dolls privacy request". State whether you request access, correction or deletion and mention Ladybird Dolls Spring Dressing.
  2. If asking about Analytics records, use the privacy-request button in the game's parent settings while Analytics is enabled, when possible. It prepares a draft with an available app-instance identifier. Alternatively include that identifier if you already have it. Do not send a child's name, photographs, passwords or advertising ID. An email address alone does not identify an installation.
  3. We will respond to establish which records and requester we can identify and help with requests for data under our control, including identifiable Analytics records and support correspondence. Google advertising data and records outside our control are handled under Google's request procedures. We explain any records retained because of legal obligations; such records are retained only as required. Local game saves and device backups must be removed using the device controls described in section 7.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond to and handle your inquiry. Correspondence is retained for as long as necessary for that purpose and any legitimate legal requirements. You may request deletion of correspondence by contacting the address above; any information that must be retained for legal reasons is handled accordingly.

Email is processed by the email providers involved in sending and receiving your message, under their privacy terms. The game itself does not collect an email address to let you play.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on the device until it is overwritten through gameplay or removed using the device's app-data controls. The game does not have an automatic time-based expiry for local saves.

A parent, guardian or device owner can delete app-private game information using Android Settings, choosing this app and clearing its storage or app data. The exact menu name varies by device. Uninstalling removes app-private data from the device; progress, coins and saved outfits may be lost. Files saved outside app-private storage and operating-system or cloud backups must be managed separately. The developer cannot remotely clear files or backups on your device.

On October 4, 2026, the game's Analytics property was configured to retain event and user data for 2 months, with renewal on new user activity disabled. Google applies setting changes after 24 hours. These controls do not affect most standard aggregated reports and do not mean that backups and all service records expire at the same time. Google determines retention of other data it processes, including advertising data. See Analytics retention controls and Google's data-retention explanation. Clearing local data does not itself erase records already processed by Google. Contact us for assistance with requests concerning data within our control.

The game has no developer game account to delete. Privacy questions and requests about correspondence or the game's data practices can be sent to gameartstudioteam@gmail.com.

8. Data handling and external services

Local game files rely on the device's app-storage protections and are not described as end-to-end encrypted storage. Protecting access to the device and managing its backups also protects those saves. Google's advertising and analytics SDKs use encrypted transmission. No online service or storage system can provide an absolute guarantee against every risk.

Opening a Google privacy page, an advertisement destination or another external website takes you to that provider's service and privacy practices. Those services may handle additional information when you choose to interact with them.

9. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the game's data practices change. The last-updated date above identifies this version. Questions about an update can be sent to our privacy contact.
